Sample workflow: insurance eligibility and benefits verification

Sample workflow with made-up member details. No real patient information.

Scenario: a patient is scheduled for an appointment, and the practice needs to verify insurance coverage before the visit.

  1. Obtain the necessary member and insurance details through authorized channels.
  2. Access the appropriate insurance payer system.
  3. Locate the member's insurance record.
  4. Verify eligibility and coverage dates.
  5. Review applicable benefits, deductibles, copayments, and coinsurance.
  6. Record the verification results.
  7. Communicate the findings to the authorized practice staff.
