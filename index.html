About me

Clear, kind support, from insurance calls to inboxes

I started my career at Teleperformance, answering health insurance questions for Blue Shield of California members. Every call taught me the same thing: people feel better when someone explains their coverage clearly and kindly.

I carried that habit into four years of remote admin work at Authority Appraisals, managing inboxes, quotations, payments, and deadlines. Now I bring that mix of healthcare knowledge and organized support to clinics and insurance teams, and I'm adding social media management for healthcare businesses.

Your first visit is easier with a few things in hand.

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card, and any questions you would like to ask. If you are not sure what else we need, send us a message and our front desk team will help.

We look forward to meeting you.

Tuesday: How to reschedule an appointment

Plans change, and that is okay.

Swipe to see how to reschedule: call or message us, tell us your name and your current appointment time, and we will find a new time that works for you. The sooner you let us know, the easier it is to find a good spot.

In this short video, our front desk team shows how we prepare for the day: confirming appointments, checking schedules, and getting everything ready so your visit starts smoothly.
